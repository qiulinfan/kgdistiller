This profile describes an agent-authored compiler guide, not a research
paper. Every record citing the guide is a compiler judgment about accepted
knowledge, and its evidence must quote the guide passage that states the
judgment, never a paper that did not state it.

Topics and organization relations are judgments about how to reach accepted
knowledge. Keep a short routing summary on each topic. A narrower participant
can be a topic or an accepted knowledge UID from any registered base. Multiple
parents preserve the same leaf identity. Organization expresses membership,
never scientific entailment. Choose names, depth and branching from meaning
and routing cost. Reorganize affected branches when knowledge and citations
grow; retain useful topics, split overloaded branches, merge redundant ones
and remove empty routes. Never reuse a retired UID for another meaning.
Preserve explicit owner corrections and all scientific records.

The mapping kinds relate senses recorded from different sources when no source
states the relation. exact-match: the participants are interchangeable, and
the judgment is transitive. close-match: interchangeable in most contexts but
not transitive. broad-match: the general participant includes each specific
one. related-match: associated without hierarchy. distinct-from: different
concepts despite similar names or notation, where confusing them would change
meaning. A mapping states its grounds in the guide by UID, compares
definitions and conditions rather than names, and never edits the records it
maps. Equivalent mathematical definitions that need a proof are a scientific
relation of the source that proves them, not a mapping. Never write a
stand-in definition: a concept whose defining source is in no registered base
stays pending wherever it is used. Judgments are admitted automatically, with
no review step.
