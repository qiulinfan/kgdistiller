This profile describes an agent-authored navigation guide, not a research
paper. Topics and organization relations are compiler judgments about how to
reach accepted knowledge. Their evidence must quote this registered guide,
never a paper that did not state the classification. Keep a short routing
summary on each topic. A narrower participant can be a topic or an accepted
knowledge UID from any registered base. Multiple parents preserve the same
leaf identity. Organization expresses membership, never scientific entailment.
A topic routes to knowledge and never stands in for a concept whose defining
source is missing. Choose names, depth and branching from meaning and routing
cost. Reorganize affected branches when knowledge and citations grow; retain
useful topics, split overloaded branches, merge redundant ones and remove
empty routes. Never reuse a retired UID for another meaning. Preserve explicit
owner corrections and all scientific records.
